04 / THE TRIANGLE COMMUNITY

Three points.
One world of opportunity.

International business grows through relationships. A businessperson in one country connects with partners, markets and investment opportunities in another. HSBC can help bring these connections together through international banking and wealth services. The red triangle becomes a symbol of that connected community — separate points, strengthened by the links between them.

PEOPLE Entrepreneurs and international clients
MARKETS Countries, partners and new markets
OPPORTUNITY Business growth and investment

03 / VISUAL SUMMARY

One thread. A world of possibilities.

The campaign uses a simple visual language to represent trust, international connections and business growth.

01. Colour palette

HSBC red creates recognition and energy, balanced with white, charcoal and light grey for a clear, confident look.

02. Typeface

Arial / Helvetica sans-serif keeps headlines bold and readable, with regular-weight text for supporting information.

03. Modules

Repeatable blocks, connecting lines and circular nodes form a flexible graphic system for posters, digital layouts and campaign touchpoints.
